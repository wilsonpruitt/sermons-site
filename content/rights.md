## What this is

Sermons preached by Wilson Pruitt, pastor of Covenant UMC Austin (formerly Bee Creek
UMC), 2020-present. Each page is a full manuscript, as preached — not a summary or an
AI-generated transcript.

## How this site is made

`sermons.wilsonpruitt.com` is a static [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) site fed by
a sync script that copies sermons — and only sermons, not board emails, funerals, or
pastoral notes — out of the author's private note vault. The publish rule requires
`type: sermon`, a date on or before today, and a real written body (a bare lectionary
citation with no sermon prose is excluded). The text on this site is exactly what was
synced from the vault; nothing here is drafted or edited by a machine.
