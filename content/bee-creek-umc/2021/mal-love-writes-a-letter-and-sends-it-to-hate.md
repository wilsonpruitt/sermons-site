**Series:** 2021

> A note inside the Easter 2021 'Works of Love' sermon series folder containing only the lyrics to Mal Blum's song 'Love and Hate.' The lyrics personify Love and Hate as two characters: Love repeatedly writes affectionate letters and sails joyfully through the sky, while Hate discards the letters, claims indifference, and walks the streets shaking hands with loners and drifters with a serious look. No sermon body or scripture is present; the song appears to be source material gathered to frame a message on love's persistence versus hate's cold indifference.
