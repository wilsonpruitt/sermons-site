**Scripture:** Romans 5:17; Colossians 1:21-22; 1 Timothy 2:5-6

> A sparse sermon note gathering scriptures on evil and salvation. It pairs Romans 5:17 (where grace and the gift of righteousness through Christ far outweigh the reign of death from Adam's trespass) with Colossians 1:21-22 (the once-hostile and evil-doing reconciled through Christ's death) and 1 Timothy 2:5-6 (Christ as the one mediator who gave himself a ransom for all). It closes with Christian Wiman's line that wounds, if tended, can let love enter—framing evil and suffering as something grace overcomes and even works through.

**Illustrations:** christian-wiman-wounds-love
