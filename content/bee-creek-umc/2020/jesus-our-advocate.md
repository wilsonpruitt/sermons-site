> An Evensong sermon on 1 John 2:1 reminding hearers that though God does not wish us to sin, mistakes happen, and we are not left alone. Jesus Christ the righteous one is our advocate, and the Greek 'Iēsoun Christon dikaion' shows that our advocate is justice itself, the Logos and Torah of God. Drawing on the preacher's own childhood guilt over stealing fruit and a recent personal mistake, the message insists that being a Christian is not about new ways of feeling guilty or inadequate. When we sin, God does not cast us out but draws closer, standing with us. The closing call: do not be afraid, for God offers life not guilt, hope not shame; may we let Jesus advocate for us.
