> An unfinished sermon (the manuscript breaks off at the third virtue) on Mark 10:13-16, with Job 1:1; 2:1-10 as the paired lectionary reading. Drawing on the historical observation that ancient cultures had no concept of childhood and saw children as immature, non-responsible, and low-status, the sermon reads Jesus's two commands: do not bar children from his presence, and receive the kingdom of God as a little child. It affirms that Covenant already welcomes children, then presses the harder claim that our whole bearing toward God should be childlike. It names three childlike virtues to appropriate -- courage (David and Goliath), humility (Joseph in slavery), and wonder -- though the text ends just as the wonder section begins.
