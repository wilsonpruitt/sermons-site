**Series:** Epiphany

> Pastor uses football's 'prevent defense' as an extended metaphor: just as teams cautiously mitigate late-game leads instead of playing boldly, we tend to merely mitigate rather than confront injustice. Drawing on Isaiah 9 and its fulfillment in Matthew 4, and on Gideon's faith-driven 300 at the 'day of Midian,' he argues the Christian response to injustice is not anxious mitigation but the light of Christ. Because Christ has done everything for us, we are freed from worry about tomorrow (Matthew 6) and freed to love the people right in front of us, breaking the oppressor's rod on our own hearts.
