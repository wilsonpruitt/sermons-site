> A Youth Sunday sermon on the 5th Sunday of Easter that frames hope for the future through Nehemiah's charge ('the joy of the LORD is your strength') and Jesus' promise of peace and a prepared place in John 14. Rather than preaching at length, the pastor turns the sermon over to the congregation's youth to answer questions they felt led to address, modeling that the way to meet anxiety about the future is to listen to, honor, and support the young people God has already called, celebrating God's ongoing work in the community (including the capital campaign).

John 14 offers a vision of our future with God. It is a passage I often read at memorial services and funerals in order to offer hope and consolation. 

Peace I give you. My peace I leave with you. I do not. leave it as the world does. Do not let your hearts be troubled. 

When we think about the future, of our world today, there's a lot of causes of trouble, anxiety, and worry. If you're retired and the majority of your natural life is behind you, you may think about the type of world your children and your children's children stepping into.

If you are younger, you may think about what world you are stepping into: where are you going to fit in the future? How will you make a difference? 

One of the most hopeful things I have found to do is to actually talk to young people. And so this morning, we're gonna do the sermon in a slightly different way, instead of me going on and on and on, I gave a set of questions to the youth, and those who are able to be here today are going to come and answer one of the questions they felt led about. 

And I'll wrap it up it the end. Instead of waiting for something, realizing that there are people already here who have been called by God. God is on their hearts. Instead of telling them what they should think, and they should do, let us imagine and celebrate the future of God's presence in this community by listening. To the youth. by honoring them. It's celebrating what God has done. Through so many of you in this capital campaign, by offering us an opportunity to support them and help guide them into the future, that they feel that too.
